Password Reset

Users can reset their password from the login page by clicking "Forgot Password".
A password reset link will be sent to the registered email address.
The password reset link expires after 30 minutes.

Account Lock

An account will be temporarily locked after five failed login attempts.
The account will automatically unlock after 15 minutes.

Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days after purchase.
Refund requests are processed within 3 to 5 business days.

Payment Issues

If a payment is successful but the balance has not been updated,
customers should wait up to 10 minutes before contacting customer support.
